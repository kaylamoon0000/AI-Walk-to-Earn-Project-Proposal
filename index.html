Bringing the AI Walk-to-Earn System to Life in the Harlem Plaza

Project Overview

AI Walk-to-Earn Harlem is a redesign proposal for a pedestrian plaza experience where AI verifies walking, recommends routes, and connects people to rewards offered by nearby businesses.

The Problem

The redesign addresses low pedestrian engagement, limited reasons to explore, weak links between walking and business activity, and the need for a safer, more active public space.

Site / Context

The Harlem plaza already has open paving, shaded areas, seating edges, and nearby storefronts. This proposal uses those conditions to guide movement, improve comfort, and place landmarks strategically.

Design Options

The final direction combines safe walking routes, AI activity recognition, landmark QR scans, and local business rewards into one hybrid system that is both practical and engaging.

Human Reflection on AI Use

AI helped with concept generation, visualizing redesign ideas, and structuring the proposal. Human judgment was needed to decide what best fits Harlem, evaluate realism, and explain the community impact.

Reflection / Conclusion

This proposal transforms the plaza into a smarter and more interactive place where movement is rewarded, local businesses gain visibility, and public space feels more purposeful and connected.
