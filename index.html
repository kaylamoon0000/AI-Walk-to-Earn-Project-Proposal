Project Title
AI Walk-to-Earn Harlem

About the Project

The goal of this project is to design a proposal website for an AI-powered system that rewards pedestrians for walking through the Harlem plaza. The concept combines public health, urban design, local business support, and location-based technology.

The essential question behind the proposal is: How can artificial intelligence encourage people to walk more while supporting local businesses in a pedestrian plaza?

The current plaza can feel underused, disconnected, and less interactive.

The Problem

Many pedestrian plazas struggle with low engagement, limited incentives to explore, uneven movement through the site, and weak connections between foot traffic and nearby businesses.

Without active use, public spaces can feel empty or unsafe. This redesign proposal responds to that problem by turning walking into a verified, rewarding, and community-centered experience.

Site / Context

This section explains how the Harlem plaza works as a real place. The redesign focuses on movement, comfort, safety, accessibility, use patterns, and local business activity.

The site analysis can show where pedestrians naturally gather, where routes feel safer, where businesses could benefit from more visibility, and where strategic landmarks with QR codes could confirm visits and encourage people to continue walking.

Design Options

These proposal directions compare different ways the Harlem plaza could be activated before selecting the final redesign.

Option 1: Route Rewards

Focus on AI walking detection, route verification, and time-based rewards for users who complete safe paths.

Option 2: Landmark Checkpoints

Add physical landmarks near businesses where pedestrians scan QR codes to confirm visits and unlock store-related incentives.

Option 3: Hybrid Experience

Combine walking routes, AI verification, landmark scans, and business rewards into one full plaza engagement system.

Final Redesign Proposal

The selected proposal is the hybrid version: an AI-guided walk-to-earn plaza experience with route verification, strategic landmarks, QR-code confirmation, and direct connections to local businesses.

Selected Proposal

Pedestrians begin a walk in the app, follow a recommended route, and earn rewards when the system confirms real walking behavior, safe route completion, and landmark check-ins.

Landmark checkpoints are placed near businesses so the visit feels physical and visible. QR scans provide stronger proof that a person reached the location, while also helping nearby businesses benefit from increased foot traffic.

This approach creates a more active plaza and turns movement into a social, economic, and urban design strategy.

Why This Proposal Works

It solves more than one problem at once: it increases walking, improves engagement, supports business visibility, and gives planners better insight into how the plaza is used.

It also creates a memorable experience because users are not only tracked digitally but also interact with real destinations in the plaza through strategically placed landmarks.

Human Reflection on AI Use

What AI Helped With

AI helped generate ideas for walking verification, reward logic, route recommendations, and visual concepts for the redesigned plaza. It also helped organize information into a clear proposal structure.

What Required Human Judgment

Human decision-making was still necessary to choose the best redesign direction, decide where landmarks should be placed, evaluate fairness and realism, and explain how the project meets community needs in Harlem.

Reflection / Conclusion

The AI Walk-to-Earn Harlem proposal reimagines the plaza as an active public environment where walking is rewarded, local businesses are supported, and public space becomes safer, smarter, and more engaging.
